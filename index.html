Where do the 15% and 10% come from?

They're rules of thumb from the Basic Financial Planning Guide by MAS and MoneySense: spend at most 15% of take-home pay on insurance protection, invest at least 10% for your goals, and keep about 6 months of expenses as an emergency fund. They're starting points, not rules for everyone.

Markets fall. That's normal.

Big falls happen every few years. Here are the biggest recent ones, and how long the S&P 500 took to get back to its old high after hitting bottom.

Crash (S&P 500)FallBack to old high
Dot-com bust, 2000 to 2002−49%about 4½ years
Global Financial Crisis, 2007 to 2009−57%about 4 years
Covid-19, 2020−34%about 5 months
Interest rate shock, 2022−25%about 15 months
Tariff shock, 2025−19%under 3 months

Investing every month through a fall means buying at sale prices. Selling in a panic is what turns a dip into a real loss. That's why money you'll need within a few years shouldn't be in the market at all.

🚩 If anyone promises guaranteed high returns, walk away. Real investments go up and down. Check the MAS Investor Alert List, and make sure whoever advises you is on the MAS Register of Representatives (mine is CSY300926263).
